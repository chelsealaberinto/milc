01 — Our Story

Education that sees the whole learner.

"Every child is born with a unique footprint of intelligence. Our mission is to help them step confidently into the world."

Founded in Tanauan City, the Multiple Intelligence Learning Center (MILC) is built upon Dr. Howard Gardner's theory of Multiple Intelligences. We understand that academic development is not a one-size-fits-all model.

Instead of asking, "How smart is this student?", we ask, "How is this student smart?" By designing our curriculum to engage the eight distinct areas of intelligence, we create paths of discovery for logical thinkers, wordsmiths, creative artists, young musicians, athletic achievers, and natural leaders alike.

Philosophy

Core Beliefs

MULTIPLE INTELLIGENCE LEARNING CENTER OF TANAUAN CITY INC. is concerned on the basic foundation of education and its continuing process to develop the young minds in moral, spiritual, intellectual and social aspects.

The institution is also committed in providing quality education through effective instruction and technologically advanced facilities to produce holistic and God-loving citizens of our nation.

Mission & Vision

Educational Commitments

Multiple Intelligence Learning Center is committed to the education of one's life. It aims to develop intellectual growth through the use of specialized materials. A positive self-concept that will equip the child to become effective, responsible communicators, critical thinkers and good problem solvers.

It provides activities that will develop fine and gross motor skills to become well-coordinated, self-help skills to become independent and self-reliant. Lastly, to develop social and interpersonal skills for healthy adjustments, attitudes and values.

"Our mission is to promote academic excellence that will provide our young learners to attain their utmost potential to develop their multiple intelligences..."

MILC is dedicated to provide its clienteles with student centered, value-driven and holistic teaching-learning processes that will develop the child to become productive citizen of our country.

2003
Foundation of MILC

Multiple Intelligence Learning Center was established in 2003. It was founded by Mr. and Mrs. Arvin Cada. Mrs. Doris Cada, in her expertise in handling different kinds of children, got the idea of establishing the name of the school.

She strongly believes that each child has a unique kind of learning ability and intelligences. She also believes on the theoretical idea of Howard Gardner on Multiple Intelligences that children should understand the world by knowing who we are and what we can do. She also formulated a unique curriculum with different approaches and strategies that will equip the child with productive learning.

First Year
First Steps & Teamwork

During the first year of operation, the school was able to raise 43 pupils from Toddler to Preparatory. Teacher Doris led the way in teaching these little children. The tandem of husband and wife earned positive response from their different experiences. Teacher Doris facilitates the academic works while Sir Arvin focuses on marketing strategies.

Growth
Rising Demands

It was a combination of hard work, intellect, patience and faith that in the succeeding years the enrollees continue to increase. The parents of the children who graduated from MILC in the preparatory level and studied in the elementary levels in other school requested Teacher Doris to put up the Elementary grades.

2010 - 2012
Elementary Expansion & Accreditation

In 2010, with the request of most of the parents, the school put up Elementary levels from grade 1 to grade 4. Grade 1 to 6 was fully established in 2012. In the years of the elementary department's existence, we are very proud to say that we have surpassed all the expectations in the field of academic and non-academic as well and once again have proven that this institution center acknowledged not only in the CALABARZON but in the whole nation.

Multiple Intelligence Learning Center will continue to search and implement innovations and modern techniques in teaching.
